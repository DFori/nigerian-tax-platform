## 1. Product Overview & Vision

The Nigerian Tax Platform is an educational, calculation, and compliance management platform designed for Nigerian individuals, sole proprietors, freelancers, and small/medium businesses (SMEs). It provides deterministic tax calculations, statutory filing deadline alerts, expense deductibility guides, and compliance checklists.

---

## 2. Special Architecture Rule: TAX RULES CHANGE

Tax laws in Nigeria (e.g. Finance Acts 2019–2023, Personal Income Tax Act [PITA], Companies Income Tax Act [CITA], Value Added Tax Act [VATA], Capital Gains Tax [CGT]) evolve periodically with statutory amendments.

### Mandatory Rule Engine Constraints:
1. **Never hardcode tax rules in arbitrary UI/API code**.
2. **Every tax rule must specify**:
   - `tax_type`: PITA (PAYE / Direct Assessment), CITA, VAT, CGT, Tertiary Education Tax (TET).
   - `jurisdiction`: Federal (FIRS) vs State Internal Revenue Service (SIRS / LIRS / etc.).
   - `effective_start_date` and `effective_end_date`.
   - `source_citation`: Official Act, Gazette number, circular URL, and verification date.
   - `rule_version`: Semantic version of the statutory rule.
3. **Information Classification**:
   - **Official Information**: Directly cited from authoritative statutory texts.
   - **Calculations**: Deterministic outputs generated by verified rule versions.
   - **Estimates**: Approximations where user input is incomplete (must be explicitly labeled as ESTIMATE).
   - **Educational**: Plain-English explanations of tax concepts and reliefs.

---

## 3. Core Capabilities

1. **Personal Income Tax (PAYE & Direct Assessment)**: Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA), statutory pension/NHF/NHIS deductions, progressive tax brackets (7% to 24%).
2. **Small Business / SME CITA**: Small business threshold (turnover < ₦25M = 0% CITA; ₦25M–₦100M = 20%; > ₦100M = 30%).
3. **VAT Calculator & Exemption List**: Basic food items, medical goods, educational materials verification.
4. **Filing Calendar & Reminders**: PAYE (10th of succeeding month), VAT (21st of succeeding month), CITA (6 months after fiscal year end).
